X For You algorithm · read from the open-source code · 14 Oct 2026

Replies and shares move your post on X. Likes barely do.

X guesses what each person will do with your post, then adds up those guesses. A reply, quote or shared link counts many times more than a like. A mute or report counts hard against you. Small accounts get a test slot in the first 2 hours. Decide three things below: when you post, how often, and where you reply.

Before you read: what we assumed

  • The numbers come from the default values in X's public code. X says a scheduled job keeps them equal to what runs live.
  • A weight multiplies a guess (how likely a person is to reply), not a count of replies. So "1 reply = 7.5 likes" is not literally true.
  • Some rules are not public (some spam rules and some prompts). X also runs tests on part of its users.
1 · The path of one post

How does X decide what to show?

It collects posts, removes old and unwanted ones, scores the rest, then removes unsafe ones.

  • Every post gets 48 hours. After that, it can't show up in For You at all.
  • The score is personal. The same post scores differently for each viewer, based on what that viewer did before.
2 · The weights

Which actions count most?

Copying your link counts most. Replies, quotes and DM shares come next. A like is worth 0.66.

  • Write posts people answer or send to a friend. A question, a hot take or a useful link does more than a nice line people only like.
  • Bait costs you. One post that makes people mute you can undo many good ones.

Formula: Final Score = Σ (weight × P(action)), in xai-value-model/scoring.rs.

3 · The small-account slot

Is there a boost for small accounts?

Yes. A new post can get one test slot in a stranger's feed, but only in its first 2 hours.

  • Stay online after you post. X picks between eligible posts by their like rate on early views. Its starting guess is about 1.5%.
  • The slot is about position 15 in the feed. It's a real chance to reach people who don't follow you.
Decision 1 of 3

When will you post?

Early replies and likes decide if the post wins the test slot.

ColdStartImpressionThreshold 200 · ColdStartMaxPostAgeSecs 7200 · ColdStartFollowerCap 50000 · ColdStartSlotMin/Max 15/16 · Thompson sampling on, Beta(0.75, 49.25) prior (home-mixer/scorers/author_cold_start.rs). The code buckets viewers into test groups, so not every viewer gets this slot.

4 · Replies and mutual follows

Do my replies reach new people?

Rarely. A reply shows in For You only to people who follow both you and the person you replied to.

  • Mutual follows are the real prize. For original posts from someone a viewer follows back, the reply weight goes from 5 to 20.
  • So use replies to make friends, and original posts to reach strangers.
Decision 2 of 3

Where will you spend your replies?

A mutual follow raises the weight of every future original post you make, for that person.

OONRetweetReplyFilter removes replies and reposts from accounts the viewer does not follow. SelfReplyChainFilter keeps a reply only if the viewer follows the account it is aimed at. BidirectionalFollowReplyWeightBoost 15.0 (5 → 20), originals only; set to 15 on 24 Jul 2026 after an A/B test (docs/BIDIRECTIONAL_BOOST_CHANGE.md).

5 · Posting in a row

What happens if I post a lot?

Each extra post a person sees from you in one visit is worth less, down to a quarter.

  • Bursts compete with themselves. Five posts in an hour mostly fight each other for the same viewers.
  • Spread posts across the day, and put your best idea in your best post.
Decision 3 of 3

How many original posts a day?

Fewer posts each get a full test slot and full score. More posts split both.

AuthorDiversityDecay 0.5, AuthorDiversityFloor 0.25: multiplier = (1 − 0.25) × 0.5ⁿ + 0.25. OonWeightFactor 0.75 (vm-ranker/params.rs).

6 · Myths

Which popular tips does the code not support?

Links, Premium and video have no special rule in the scoring code.

  • Spend energy on what people do with your post, not on format tricks.
  • Check X's "Under the Hood" page to see if any label limits your account.
Limits

What we still do not know

  • Live values can differ for some users. X runs tests, and the code shows test groups.
  • The model's inputs are not fully visible. Things like Premium status may matter inside the model.
  • Some spam rules are hidden on purpose, to stop people gaming them.
